Intended use

Terms of use

Unified Email Manager is an operator-controlled personal application. It is not offered here as a public hosted service.

Access and operation

This website provides information about the application. It does not offer public accounts, subscriptions, purchases or mailbox access. The operator installs the application, connects accounts they are authorized to use, and controls its enabled features.

Permissions and decisions

Email rules and workflow approvals determine which operations may run. Calendar event access is authorized separately from calendar reading. Assistant suggestions and drafts require review; they are not a substitute for checking source emails or calendars.

Availability and recovery

Operation depends on the computer being awake, its network connection, provider availability and valid account authorization. A reboot may require an operating-system sign-in to unlock the credential store. Failed or uncertain operations may require review before continuing.

Privacy and support

The privacy information describes data access, local storage, backups and optional sharing. Use the support email shown on Google’s consent screen to contact the operator about the application.

Personal-use application information. These pages do not connect to mailboxes or collect sign-in credentials.
