Unified Email Manager

A personal application for reviewing connected mailboxes, applying approved email rules, and bringing selected calendars into one local dashboard.

This website describes the application. It does not host the dashboard, provide public registration, or request access to a visitor’s Google account.

What the application does

The locally installed manager reads connected email accounts, keeps Inbox previews, and applies rules approved by its operator. Authorized actions can move messages to Archive or Trash and update read flags. Uncertain actions require review.

The calendar view reads selected calendars. A separate Google permission supports the application’s reviewed event workflows on calendars covered by that authorization.

Optional assistance and notifications

Optional assistance uses a locally installed model to help review messages and prepare drafts. Private Telegram notifications and requested assistant responses share information with Telegram only when the operator enables the relevant features.

Google access and local storage

Google authorization takes place on Google’s own sign-in pages. Refresh credentials are kept in the operating system’s credential store. Cached information, rules and workflow records are stored on the operator’s computer.

Read the privacy and data-use information and terms of use for more detail. For support, use the operator’s support address displayed on the Google consent screen.
